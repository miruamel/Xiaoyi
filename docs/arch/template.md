## Guidelines for Writing ADRs

1. **One ADR per decision** - Don't bundle unrelated decisions
2. **Write in present tense** - "We use..." not "We will use..."
3. **Be specific** - Avoid vague language
4. **Include trade-offs** - Every decision has costs
5. **Link related ADRs** - Build a knowledge graph
6. **Number sequentially** - 0001, 0002, etc.
7. **Don't delete** - Mark as Superseded instead
8. **Keep it short** - 1-2 pages max
